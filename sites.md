### ### How to Find Hidden RSS Feeds

If a site doesn't prominently display an RSS icon, try these tricks:

1. **The "Feed" Suffix:** Add `/feed` or `/rss` to the end of the URL (e.g., `example.com/feed`). This works on almost all WordPress sites.
2. **View Page Source:** Right-click on a webpage, select **View Page Source**, and search (**Ctrl+F**) for "RSS" or "Atom". Look for a link ending in `.xml`.
3. **Google News as a Feed:** You can turn any Google News search into an RSS feed. Just search for a topic on Google News, scroll to the bottom, and click "Create Alert" or use a generator like **RSS.app**.
